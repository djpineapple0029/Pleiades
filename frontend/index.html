Click or Enter to fly · ? keys

Click to fly

  • WASDmove
  • Spaceup
  • Shiftdown
  • Mouselook
  • Double-clicknew node in open space
  • Hold rightmenu on the targeted node or edge, move to choose
  • Enterrename the targeted node or edge; Enter saves, Esc cancels
  • Ctrl/⌘Enteredit the targeted node's notes
  • Nnotes sidebar on/off
  • /find a star by name; Enter flies there
  • Backspacefly back to where you were before the last jump
  • Bbalance the layout, press again to stop
  • Taboverview: orbit the whole map, press again to fly
  • Ctrl/⌘Ssave to a file
  • Ctrl/⌘Oopen a file
  • Ctrl/⌘Eexport a view-only .html anyone can open
  • Escrelease pointer
  • ?show or hide this list while the pointer is free
