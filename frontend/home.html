A mind map you fly through.

Every idea is a star. Link the ones that go together and they gather into coloured clusters. Then you fly around the whole thing in 3D with the keyboard and mouse, the way you'd move in a game.

Get started No account needed. You save your map as a file.

How it works

  1. Drop a star

    Double-click anywhere in empty space and a star appears where you're looking. Press Enter to name it, or Ctrl/⌘+Enter to write longer notes inside it.

  2. Link two stars

    Hold the right mouse button on a star and a ring menu opens. Move onto Connect and let go, then click the star it should link to. Links can have names too, so “caused by” or “see also” can sit right on the line.

  3. Watch the clusters form

    Stars that are closely linked get grouped, and each group gets its own colour. You don't sort anything into folders. The groups come from what you've connected, so they shift as the map grows.

  4. Fly around it

    W A S D to move, the mouse to look, the scroll wheel for speed. Click a star and only its links stay lit. Press / to find a star by name and fly straight to it, or Tab to pull back and see the whole map at once.

  5. Tidy it up

    Once it gets tangled, press B and the map rearranges itself into a tree hanging off its main stars. Hold T to choose the shape: a cone you can fly into, or flat like a chart.

Where your maps live

In a file

Ctrl/⌘+S saves the map as an .atlasmap file on your computer. Give it a password and the file is encrypted, so nobody can open it without that password, including whoever runs this server. If you forget the password, there's no way back in.

In an account

Sign in and your maps sit on this server in a list. They save themselves a few seconds after you stop, and older versions are kept so you can go back. One thing to know: they're stored unencrypted, so the person who runs the server could read them.

Either way, Ctrl/⌘+E exports a view-only .html file you can send to anyone. It opens in any browser; they can fly around your map but can't change it.

All the keys

You can bring this list up in the app too: press ? while the pointer is free.
